## Notes

**Distance** is comoving (proper) distance — where the object actually is today, accounting for the expansion of space since the light was emitted.

**Light travel time** is lookback time — how long the photons have been traveling. For nearby objects the two are nearly identical; for distant objects they diverge significantly because space has been expanding throughout the journey.

**Redshift (z)** measures how much the universe has expanded since the light was emitted. z = 0 is today; z = 1100 is the CMB. For objects beyond ~1 Gly, z is noted in the description where relevant.

**Unit key**
- AU — astronomical unit (Earth–Sun distance, ~150 million km)
- ly — light-year
- Mly — million light-years
- Gly — billion light-years
